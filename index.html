Ringhold

Two crowns, one valley. Spend your coins, raise the walls, and hold the keep until dawn.

Settings

How it plays

  • Coins are your only tool. Look at a stone plot or wall stakes and hold the drop key to pay.
  • Plots take a house (coins each dawn), an archer tower or a brazier. Walls upgrade from wood to stone to iron.
  • Hire archers at the bow rack beside the keep. Archers only shoot what light reveals.
  • The kingdom is one long valley. Masklings pour in from the portals at both ends each night. They steal coins, then crowns. A partner can carry a fallen crown back to the keep before dawn.
  • Your lantern burns oil at night. Stand near the keep or a brazier to refill it. A flare scares thieves into dropping what they took.
  • Every fifth night the moon turns red. Seal both portals with coins to free the valley.

On a phone: your left thumb moves you (push to the edge to run), drag anywhere on the right to look, and hold the coin button to pay. Tap an icon in the build wheel to choose it. Turn the phone sideways for the widest view. Split-screen needs a keyboard or gamepads, so phones play alone.

Playing alone, a gamepad drives the first crown. Playing together, the first gamepad drives the second crown and a second gamepad can drive the first. Online co-op is not in this build yet; split-screen shares one screen and keyboard. Needs a keyboard and mouse or a gamepad.
